## 1. NHÓM NPC MARK CHÍNH (4 NPC cốt truyện)

### 🟢 Gundren Rockseeker (Dwarf, người thuê party)
- **Vai trò**: Patron, có bản đồ Wave Echo Cave, bị bắt cóc ở Cragmaw Castle
- **Tính cách**: Háo hức, bí mật về mỏ, bạn của Barthen
- **Dialogue thật** (sau khi cứu ở Cragmaw Castle, đoạn 1047):
  > "Gundren offers the characters his thanks and asks that they escort him back to Phandalin and then venture to Wave Echo Cave to learn the fate of his brothers, Nundro and Tharden. He knows that someone called the Black Spider orchestrated his capture and hopes to see the villain brought to justice."
- **Quest hook**: Hộ tống Gundren → Wave Echo Cave (main quest final)

### 🟢 Sildar Hallwinter (Human fighter, Lords' Alliance)
- **Vai trò**: Cứu từ Cragmaw Hideout (Part 1), agent của Lords' Alliance
- **Stats thật** (Appendix B, đoạn 1689-1704):
  - AC 16 (chainmail), HP 27 (5d8+5), STR 13, DEX 10, CON 12
  - Attacks: Longsword +3 (1d8+1), Heavy Crossbow +2 (1d10)
  - Reaction: Parry (+1d6 AC khi bị melee hit)
  - CR 1 (200 XP)
- **Dialogue thật** (đoạn 224, khi cứu):
  > "Sildar tells the characters that he intends to continue on to Phandalin, since it's the nearest settlement. He offers to pay the party 50 gp to provide escort."
- **3 Quests** (đoạn 387-389, khi ở townmaster's hall):
  1. **Finding Cragmaw Castle** — reward **500 gp** (định vị + đánh bại chieftain)
  2. **Finding Iarno** — reward **200 gp** (tìm Iarno Albrek, "a short, dark-bearded human wizard in his thirties")
  3. **Bring law to Phandalin** — tuyển vào Lords' Alliance (title: **Cloak**)

### 🔴 Nezznar / The Black Spider (Drow wizard, main villain)
- **Vai trò**: Boss cuối Wave Echo Cave, chủ mưu
- **Stats thật** (Appendix B, đoạn 1589-1610):
  - Medium humanoid (elf), neutral evil
  - AC 11 (14 with mage armor), HP 27 (6d8)
  - STR 9, DEX 13, CON 10, INT 16, WIS 14, CHA 13
  - **Spellcasting**: 4th-level wizard (save DC 13, +5 spell attack)
    - Cantrips: mage hand, ray of frost, shocking grasp
    - 1st level (4 slots): mage armor, magic missile, shield
    - 2nd level (3 slots): invisibility, suggestion
  - Innate: dancing lights (at will), darkness, faerie fire (1/day each)
  - **Spider Staff**: melee +1 (1d6-1 bludgeoning + 1d6 poison)
  - CR 2 (450 XP)
- **Tính cách**: Devious, scheming (drow đặc trưng)
- **Bức thư cho Iarno** (đoạn 603-609 — quan trọng, reveal villain):
  > "Lord Albrek,
  > My spies in Neverwinter tell me that strangers are due to arrive in Phandalin. They could be working for the dwarves. Capture them if you can, kill them if you must, but don't allow them to upset our plans. See that any dwarven maps in their possession are delivered to me with haste. I'm counting on you, Iarno. Don't disappoint me."

### 🔴 King Grol (Bugbear, Cragmaw Castle)
- **Vai trò**: Thủ lĩnh Cragmaw tribe, giữ Gundren + bản đồ
- **Tính cách**: Brutal, posts sentries, appearance abandoned (đoạn 907)
- **Lối chơi thay thế** (đoạn 892): Player có thể mặc áo choàng Redbrand + giả làm emissary của Iarno để gặp Grol

## 2. NPC THỊ TRẤN PHANDALIN (11 NPC + dialogue thật)

### 🏠 Toblen Stonehill — Innkeeper (Stonehill Inn)
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 334): Short, friendly young human male, native of Triboar. Came to Phandalin to prospect but realized he knew more about running an inn. **Upset about Redbrands** but fears retaliation against wife and children.
- **Rumors tại quán** (đoạn 335-339, invaluable cho quest hooks):
  - **Narth** (old farmer): "Sister Garaele recently left town for a few days, then returned wounded and exhausted."
  - **Elsa** (gossipy barmaid): "Daran Edermath, the orchard keeper, is a former adventurer."
  - **Lanar** (miner): "Orc raiders have been seen on the east end of Triboar Trail. The town master is looking for someone to run them off."
  - **Trilena** (innkeeper's wife): "Thel Dendrar stood up to the Redbrands a ten day ago... The ruffians murdered him. ...his wife, daughter, and son have gone missing too."
  - **Pip** (Toblen's son): "Qelline Alderleaf's son Carp said he found a secret tunnel in the woods, but Redbrands almost caught him."
  - **Freda** (weaver): "The Redbrands hassle every business in town, except for the Phandalin Miner's Exchange. They don't want trouble with Halia Thornton."

### 🏪 Elmar Barthen — Owner Barthen's Provisions
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 341): Lean and balding human male, fifty years, kindly manner. Clerks: Ander + Thistle.
- **Shop**: Biggest trading post. Stocks ordinary goods (no weapons/armor > 25 gp). Weapons/armor → Lionshield Coster.
- **Dialogue thật**:
  - Khi giao supplies (hook "Meet Me in Phandelver"): pays **10 gp each character**
  - Về Gundren (đoạn 341): "saddened by the news and encourages the party to find and rescue the dwarf. He considers Gundren a friend and was excited by talk of discovering the lost mine."
  - Về Rockseeker brothers (đoạn 342): "two more Rockseeker brothers, Nundro and Tharden, are camped somewhere outside town. Barthen hasn't seen them in a ten day." *(Tharden đã chết, Nundro là tù nhân ở Wave Echo Cave)*
  - Business news (đoạn 342): "the Redbrands are making it hard on everyone, shaking down local businesses... If the characters seem of a mind to do something about it, he tells them that the Redbrands frequent the Sleeping Giant tap house."

### ⚔️ Linene Graywind — Master Lionshield Coster
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 358): Sharp-tongued human woman, thirty-five.
- **Shop**: Armor + weapons in back room. **Won't sell to Redbrands** or anyone she thinks might threaten town.
- **Dialogue thật** (đoạn 359): "warns the characters that the ruffians are trouble and advises them to avoid the Sleeping Giant tap house."
- **Quest**: Trả lại stolen goods (từ Cragmaw area 8) → reward **50 gp** + friendship (đoạn 360)

### 🌳 Daran Edermath — Retired adventurer (Edermath Orchard)
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 347): Fit, silver-haired **half-elf** well over 100 years old. Former fighter/marshal from Dragon Coast. Member **Order of the Gauntlet**.
- **Dialogue thật** (đoạn 348): "concerned about the Redbrands... time someone took a stand against the Redbrands' leader, Glasstaff. ...the main Redbrand safe house lies under Tresendar Manor, the ruin at the east edge of town."
- **Quest: Old Owl Trouble** (đoạn 349): "prospectors in the hills northeast of Phandalin... someone is digging around in the ruins known as Old Owl Well. ...several prospectors have reported being chased from the area by undead. ...the ruins are an old watchtower of an ancient magical empire known as Netheril."
- **Reward path**: Giải quyết Redbrands + Old Owl Well → tuyển vào Order of the Gauntlet (title: **Chevalier**)

### 💰 Halia Thornton — Guildmaster Miner's Exchange (Zhentarim)
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 363): Ambitious and calculating human woman. Agent của **Zhentarim**. Working to bring Phandalin under her control.
- **Dialogue thật**:
  - "doesn't know the location of Cragmaw Castle, but she has heard that the Redbrands have a goblin minion serving them. She suggests the goblin might know the location."
  - **Quest: Halia's Job Offer** (đoạn 363): "offers the characters 100 gp to eliminate the Redbrand leader, whom the outlaws call Glasstaff, and bring her any correspondence found in the leader's quarters."
  - *(Secret: DC 15 Wisdom (Insight) check reveals ulterior motives — she wants to take over the Redbrand operation herself)*
- **Reward path**: Dispose of Redbrand leader → tuyển vào Zhentarim (title: **Fang**)

### 🌾 Qelline Alderleaf — Halfling farmer
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 368): Wise female halfling of forty-five, pragmatic, knows everything in town. Kind host (cho ngủ hayloft).
- **Carp's Story** (đoạn 368): Con trai Carp, 10 tuổi, "found a secret tunnel in a thicket [near Tresendar Manor]. A couple of 'big ugly bandits' came out of the tunnel... met with a pair of Redbrands." Tunnel → area 8 Redbrand hideout.
- **Quest: Reidoth the Druid** (đoạn 368): "longtime friend of a druid named Reidoth... 'since there's not an inch of the land he doesn't know.' ...Reidoth recently set out for the ruins of a town called Thundertree, just west of the Neverwinter Wood. The ruins are about fifty miles northwest of Phandalin."

### ⛪ Sister Garaele — Elf cleric of Tymora (Harper)
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 370): Scholarly acolyte, zealous young elf, despairs of ridding Phandalin of Redbrands. Member **Harpers**.
- **Quest: The Banshee's Bargain** (đoạn 370-373):
  - "superiors asked her to undertake a delicate mission. They wanted her to persuade a banshee named Agatha to answer a question about a spellbook."
  - "Garaele sought out Agatha in her lair, but the creature did not appear for her."
  - Cần intermediary mang **jeweled silver comb** → Agatha, hỏi về **Bowgentle's spellbook**.
  - "believes that a character who flatters Agatha's vanity might be able to trade the comb for an answer."
  - **Reward**: 3 potions of healing
- **Reward path**: Help Garaele → tuyển vào Harpers (title: **Watcher**)

### 🏛️ Harbin Wester — Townmaster
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 384): Male human banker, "a fat, pompous old fool. Completely intimidated by the Redbrands, he claims that they're 'just a mercenary guild, and not all that much trouble, really.'"
- **Notice trên board** (đoạn 381-383):
  > "REWARD—Orcs near Wyvern Tor! Those of a mind to face the orc menace should inquire within."
- **Quest: Orc Trouble** (đoạn 386): "looking for someone to head east on the Triboar Trail, where travelers have reported trouble with a band of orcs near Wyvern Tor. He offers **100 gp** to any group that can take care of the problem."
- **Jail**: 2 cells trong cellar, Harbin mang keys.

### 🔴 Iarno "Glasstaff" Albrek — Wizard, Redbrand leader
- **Vai trò**: Boss phụ Part 2, từng là Lords' Alliance member (phản bội)
- **Tính cách**: "a short, dark-bearded human wizard in his thirties" (mô tả Sildar, đoạn 389)
- **Weapon**: **Spider Staff** (melee +1, 1d6-1 bludgeoning + 1d6 poison) + **Staff of Defense**
- **Treasure** (đoạn 611-612): 180 sp, 130 gp, 5 carnelians (10gp), 2 peridots (15gp), 1 pearl (100gp), scroll of charm person, scroll of fireball, **staff of defense**
- **Letter từ Black Spider** (đoạn 603-609 — reveal cốt truyện, xem phần Nezznar)
- **XP** (đoạn 613): 200 XP nếu giết, **400 XP nếu capture** + giao cho Sildar

### 👤 Mirna Dendrar (+ children Nars, Nilsa) — Captives
- **Vai trò**: Cứu khỏi Redbrand slave pens (area 5). Vợ/con của Thel Dendrar (woodcarver bị Redbrand giết).

## 3. NPC VÙNG HOANG DÃ

### 🌲 Reidoth — Druid (Thundertree, Emerald Enclave)
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 749): "gaunt, white-bearded human who doesn't use two words when one word will do. Reasonably hospitable."
- **Vai trò**: Biết vị trí **Cragmaw Castle** + **Wave Echo Cave**
- **Dialogue thật** (đoạn 752): "If the characters ask about Cragmaw Castle, Reidoth gladly provides directions."
- **Member**: Emerald Enclave (preserve natural order)
- **Reward path**: Help chase off Venomfang → tuyển vào Emerald Enclave
- **Lưu ý** (đoạn 753): Nếu attack → biến thành gray squirrel + chạy qua crack in wall

### 👻 Agatha — Banshee (Conyberry)
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 651-657): "She might have been beautiful once, but a hateful expression twists her features now."
- **Dialogue thật** khi appear (đoạn 656-657):
  > "Foolish mortals, what do you want here? Do you not know it is death to seek me out?"
- **Quy tắc deal** (đoạn 658-659):
  - Rude/disrespectful/threatening → **scowls + disappears** (không attack, không return)
  - Respectful + polite → DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion). Roleplay tốt → advantage.
  - Có **silver comb** (từ Sister Garaele) → **tự động thành công**
- **Câu hỏi**: Vị trí **Bowgentle's spellbook**

### 🐉 Venomfang — Young Green Dragon (Thundertree tower)
- **Vai trò**: Optional boss, Deadly encounter cho level 3 party
- **Tính cách** (đoạn 788-790): "recently claimed the tower, having passed over Thundertree while searching Neverwinter Wood for a suitable lair."
- **Behavior** (đoạn 790): "does not want to give up such a promising lair, but if the characters reduce the dragon to half its hit points, it climbs to the top [and flees]"
- **Treasure** (đoạn 791): 800 sp, 150 gp, 4 silver goblets w/ moonstones (60gp each), scroll of misty step, scroll of lightning bolt
- **XP** (đoạn 792): **2,000 XP** nếu drive away, **3,900 XP** nếu slay
